## 🏛️ GEORGIAN GOVERNMENT PROGRAMS

### Innovation and Technology Agency (ITA)
- **Startup Georgia**: Up to ₾100,000 — seed funding for tech startups (Annual calls)
- **Matching Grant Program**: ₾50,000-200,000 — government matches private investment
- **Innovation Vouchers**: ₾5,000-15,000 — R&D and prototyping
- **Tech Park Residency**: Free office + mentorship (Ongoing)

### Enterprise Georgia
- **Startup**: Up to ₾100,000 — 90% grant, 10% co-financing (Annual Q1)
- **Export Promotion**: Up to ₾150,000 — for international expansion
- **Co-financing**: Up to ₾500,000 — 50% government match

### Digital Georgia
- E-governance tenders for digital service providers
- Data protection compliance framework (market opportunity)

## 🌍 USAID GEORGIA

### Economic Security Program (~$25M total, 2022-2027)
- Grants: $25,000-$250,000 for tech/SME companies
- Focus: SME competitiveness, digital economy
- Check: sam.gov

### Governing for Growth (G4G) (~$15M)
- Sub-grants for digital governance solutions
- Focus: E-governance, regulatory reform

### Civil Society Engagement Program (~$5M)
- Grants: $10,000-$75,000 for civic tech
- Focus: Digital tools for CSOs

## 🇩🇪 GIZ (German Development)

### Private Sector Development South Caucasus (~€20M)
- Grants: €10,000-€100,000 for digital transformation
- Deadline: Annual calls Q2-Q3

### Digital Transformation Eastern Partnership (~€15M)
- Grants: €50,000-€200,000 for digital projects
- Focus: E-governance, cybersecurity

## 🇺🇳 UNDP GEORGIA

### Innovation Facility
- Grants: $50,000-$300,000 per project
- Calls: Usually Q1 and Q3

### Accelerator Lab Georgia
- Small grants: $5,000-$30,000
- Focus: Grassroots innovation, data solutions

### EU4Digital (UNDP partner)
- Grants: €25,000-€150,000 for digital service providers
