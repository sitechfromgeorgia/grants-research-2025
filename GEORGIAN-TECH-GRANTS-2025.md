## 🏛️ 1. GEORGIAN GOVERNMENT PROGRAMS

### 1.1 Innovation and Technology Agency (ITA) — ინოვაციების და ტექნოლოგიების სააგენტო
**Website:** https://techpark.ge/en
**Focus:** Tech startups, digital innovation, IT education

| Program | Amount | Deadline | Details |
|---------|--------|----------|---------|
| **Startup Georgia** | Up to ₾100,000 | Rolling/Annual | Seed funding for early-stage tech startups. Co-financing required. |
| **Matching Grant Program** | ₾50,000-200,000 | Annual calls | Government matches private investment in tech companies |
| **Tech Park Residency** | Free office + mentorship | Ongoing | Residency at Technology Park with networking, events |
| **IT Education Vouchers** | Up to ₾5,000/person | Periodic | Training subsidies for IT staff development |
| **Innovation Vouchers** | ₾5,000-15,000 | Periodic | For R&D activities, prototyping, digital transformation |

**SiTech relevance:** HIGH — Startup Georgia and Matching Grant directly applicable for SaaS products (n8n Chatter, asistent.ge, BrainStorm Platform)

### 1.2 Enterprise Georgia — საწარმო საქართველო
**Website:** https://enterprise.gov.ge/en
**Focus:** SME development, export promotion, digital transformation

| Program | Amount | Deadline | Details |
|---------|--------|----------|---------|
| **Startup** | Up to ₾100,000 | Annual calls (usually Q1) | 90% grant, 10% co-financing for startups |
| **Micro and Small Business** | Up to ₾150,000 | Rolling | For established businesses expanding — equipment, marketing |
| **Export Promotion** | Up to ₾150,000 | Annual calls | For companies looking to export digital services internationally |
| **Digital Transformation** | Varies | Periodic | Subsidies for digital tools adoption (relevant for SaaS clients) |
| **Co-financing** | Up to ₾500,000 | Annual | Government co-finances up to 50% of business expansion projects |

**SiTech relevance:** HIGH — Export Promotion for SaaS internationalization; Startup program for new products

### 1.3 Digital Georgia — ციფრული საქართველო
**Website:** https://digital.gov.ge
**Focus:** E-governance, digital public services, data protection

| Program | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **E-Governance Development** | Government tenders for digital service providers |
| **Data Protection Framework** | Compliance requirements creating market opportunity |
| **Digital Identity** | Integration opportunities for SaaS providers |
| **Open Data Portal** | API/data access for building digital services |

**SiTech relevance:** HIGH — Compliance services (data protection), e-governance contracts, digital identity integration for SaaS products

1. **Emphasize EU alignment** — Georgia's EU candidacy makes EU-related grants very competitive
2. **Data protection angle** — Georgia adapting GDPR-like framework creates compliance market
3. **Employment impact** — Government grants love job creation numbers
4. **Export potential** — Highlight international scalability of SaaS products
5. **Innovation narrative** — AI/multi-agent systems are novel in Georgian context
6. **Co-financing ready** — Have matching funds documented (even in-kind)
7. **Track record** — Portfolio of live projects (sitech.ge clients) demonstrates capability
